Hiring Smart

6 questions to ask before hiring an electrician or plumber

A five-minute conversation saves you from a bad job. Before work starts, ask:

  • What's included in the price? Parts, labour, or both?
  • Is there a call-out or inspection fee? Get it clear upfront.
  • How long have you done this work? Experience shows in the finish.
  • Do you guarantee the work? Good pros stand behind their fixes.
  • Will you show the old/replaced parts? Keeps everyone honest.
  • Can I pay after I'm satisfied? WeAid holds payment until the job's done.
