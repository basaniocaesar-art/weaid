Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated · WeAid is operated by ,

You can cancel from your tracking page (the link we sent when you booked) or by calling WeAid.

When you cancelWhat you get back
Before a pro is assigned100%
A pro is assigned but hasn't set off100%
Your pro is already on the wayEverything except ₹99, which goes to the pro for their travel
The pro has arrived or the work has startedThe booking can't be cancelled — please report a problem instead and we'll sort it out
WeAid can't find a pro, or the pro doesn't turn up100%, and we'll offer to rebook

Not happy with the work

Report it within 48 hours from your tracking page or by phone. If the work wasn't done properly, WeAid will send a pro back to fix it at no extra charge. Where that isn't possible or doesn't solve it, WeAid will offer a partial or full refund depending on what was done.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the way you paid. Card, UPI and net-banking refunds through Razorpay usually reach you in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. If you paid WeAid directly by UPI, we send the refund by UPI within 2 working days.

Site visits

Site visits and written quotes for bigger jobs are free. If you go ahead, the agreed quote is what you pay.
