How to install
  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open WeAid Pro.
  4. Log in with your mobile number and the 6-digit code WeAid sent you — or sign up.
  5. Allow location and notifications so jobs can reach you.
